Home search

Comprehensive support in finding accommodation

We will find furnished or unfurnished rental apartments and houses for you that exactly meet your requirements. You will receive a selection of suitable offers - with photos if you wish.

After your pre-selection, we organize accompanied viewings so that you can get to know your new home in peace.

Our service:

  • Online consultation before the search begins
  • Review and optimization of your documents
  • Organization and coordination of viewing appointments
  • Communication with real estate agents
  • Accompaniment, interpreting and advice during viewings
  • Management and submission of your applications
  • Contract review and brief summary
  • Coordination of contract signing
  • Support and interpreting during the handover of the apartment
  • Preparation of a summary of the handover protocol

Bookable extras:

  • Driving service to viewings
  • Full written translation of the tenancy agreement
  • Professional cleaning of the apartment before moving in
  • Supervision on moving day
  • Filling your fridge before you move in
