Visa & Immigration

The first step in the successful relocation of an employee is the residence permit or visa - the application for which can be a complicated and time-consuming process.

We:

  • Complete the necessary forms and applications according to your specifications
  • Support you in obtaining and forwarding all the necessary documents and papers
  • Coordinate appointments and accompany you to the authorities
  • Coordinate the time schedule
  • Assist with the necessary local registration with the authorities

During the process, 1source-consulting employees work closely with the HR department and the relevant authorities.

Your Advantages

Support from employees with many years of experience and excellent knowledge of all procedures; this saves you time and efort.

These steps are part of the relocation process

The risk of additional costs arising from missing documents and records is minimized.
